Patient 3 was admitted to hospital on 4/06/2026 and presented with 3 day worsening wheeze, cough and shortness of breath made worse by a viral cold. He was diagnosed with acute asthma exacerbation (moderate-acute severe), viral-triggered. On admission he was treated with salbutamol nebulisers (back-to-back initially) with ipratropium. Prednisolone 40 mg OD commenced (5-day course). Good response; PEF improved to 78%, weaned to inhaler with spacer. Inhaler technique reviewed and corrected. He is medically suitable for discharge and will go home with prednisolone 40 mg OD (complete 5 days) and salbutamol inhaler PRN via spacer.
Advised to come back if things get worse.
