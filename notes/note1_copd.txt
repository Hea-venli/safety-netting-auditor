Patient 1 was admitted to hospital on 24/06/2026 with increased shortness of breath and more frequent coughing. She was diagnosed with an infective exacerbation of COPD. During admission she was treated with short-acting bronchodilators, oral corticosteroids and antibiotics. She uses BiPAP at home and has been advised to continue on the same settings. She is to complete her 5-day course of steroids and her antibiotic course at home. The respiratory team reviewed her inhaler technique for optimal drug delivery, and she has been referred for pulmonary rehabilitation.
Safety-netting: Return to A&E (call 999 in an emergency) if shortness of breath or frequent coughing is not relieved by her rescue pack. Contact GP for any other concerns.
